Ridge Fundastead reviews and questions from new traders

Eight questions, eight plain-English answers — and every one of them sourced from the support inbox over the past month. If your question is more specific than the templates below, drop it to the desk and we will publish the answer in the next refresh.

Sign-up takes a few minutes. Fill in the secure form on the home page with your full name, email and Australian mobile, verify your identity, and fund your account from a minimum of AUD 250. After that, the dashboard, the AI co-pilot and the demo workspace are immediately available.

We use 256-bit SSL, two-factor authentication and segregated wallets in cold storage. Funds and personal data move only through verified payment partners audited against Australian privacy and AML standards. Account ownership stays with you at all times.

Withdrawal requests are processed within one to three business days. Fee and timing previews are shown before you confirm the request, so the final amount that lands in your bank or wallet is never a surprise.

No prior experience is required. The dashboard ships with a guided tour, a risk-free demo workspace and short video lessons. Beginners typically start by mirroring AI signals and graduate to manual strategies as confidence grows.

POLi, BPAY, Visa, Mastercard and direct AUD bank transfer are supported, alongside major e-wallets and stablecoin deposits. All deposits clear with full transaction trace for tax reporting.

65+ instruments are available, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Solana, gold, silver, the ASX 200 index, AUD/USD, EUR/USD and CFDs on global equities. New listings appear in the dashboard alerts.

You choose the level of automation. The AI surfaces ideas in advisory mode, executes pre-approved templates in semi-automatic mode, or runs full algorithmic strategies inside risk caps you define.

Position-size limits, daily loss caps and cooling-off timers help you stay disciplined. Educational content and live broker chat are one tap away whenever you need a second opinion.
