Welcome to Plata Invest CA. The sign-up form below is the fastest route into the workspace — leave your details, confirm your email and our Canadian onboarding team will help you connect a broker account so you can move from the dashboard tour to your first structured trade in a single sitting.

By continuing you confirm you have read and accept our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions, and agree to be contacted about your account.

What happens after you sign up

  1. Check your inbox for the welcome email and confirm your address.
  2. A Canadian onboarding specialist calls to walk through the workspace and broker linking.
  3. You set your risk preferences, default position size and notification rules.
  4. You open the dashboard and place your first practice trade or live order — your call.

Why open a Plata Invest CA account

  • One workspace for crypto, FX, equities and CFD ideas — no tab juggling.
  • AI-led analytics that learn from the instruments you actually watch.
  • Built-in risk controls and exposure dashboards keep every position in check.
  • Local support team that understands the Canadian tax and trading landscape.
