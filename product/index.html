The Ridge Fundastead platform under the hood

The dashboard ships with three pillars: a real-time charting engine, an AI co-pilot that explains its calls in plain English, and an execution layer with hard risk caps. Together they cover the full journey from idea to filled order.

Real-time charts

Multi-pane charting with depth-of-book overlays, candles down to one second, and 80+ studies. Layouts are saved to the cloud so they follow you across devices.

AI co-pilot

A model trained on tick-level data flags breakouts, range traps and macro news. Every signal includes the rationale, the historical hit rate and a one-click "size and place" action.

Execution & risk

Smart order routing reaches the deepest pool first, while position-size caps, daily loss limits and cool-down timers run as hard guardrails — nothing is left to discipline alone.

How the Ridge Fundastead platform fits an Australian workflow

Local timezone, local payments, local language. The workspace was tuned for the way investors in Australia actually trade — small starting capital, fast switches between manual and automated workflows, and a heavy emphasis on educational scaffolding for beginners.

AEST sessions

Pre-set workspaces follow Sydney's open and close, with extended-hours flags on ASX-correlated assets.

AUD reporting

Every P&L line, fee and statement is denominated in AUD with optional USD/EUR shadow columns.

Local payments

POLi, BPAY, AUD bank transfer, Visa, Mastercard — funded accounts go live in minutes, not days.

Tax-ready exports

Trade history exports to CSV and PDF in the format your accountant expects at end of financial year.
