These Conditions of Use govern your access to the Ridge Fundastead website and the trading workspace. By opening an account or submitting any form on the site you agree to the terms below. The terms are written for residents of Australia and apply alongside the Privacy Policy.

Eligibility for Ridge Fundastead Australia

You must be at least 18 years old, hold valid government-issued identification and be a resident of Australia. Accounts opened with inaccurate details may be paused until the discrepancy is resolved.

Your account responsibilities

You are responsible for keeping the password to your account secure, for enabling the two-factor option and for reporting unusual activity within 24 hours. Sharing access with a third party is not permitted.

Deposits, withdrawals and fees

Deposits are credited once cleared by the payment provider. Withdrawals are paid to the same method used for the deposit where possible. The full fee schedule is available inside the dashboard and on the Offer page.

Acceptable use of the Ridge Fundastead platform

  • No reverse engineering, scraping or load testing of the workspace.
  • No attempts to circumvent risk caps or geographic restrictions.
  • No misuse of the support desk for harassment or fraudulent claims.
  • No automated trading agents that bypass platform rate-limits.

Trading risk disclosure

Trading FX, CFDs and cryptocurrencies carries a high level of risk to your capital. Leveraged products can lose value rapidly and may exceed the initial deposit on certain instruments. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future returns. Only commit funds you can afford to lose, and seek independent financial advice if you are unsure whether a product is appropriate.

Responsible trading commitments

We provide loss-cap, position-size and cool-down tools at no extra cost. If you feel that your trading is becoming compulsive, you can self-exclude from the dashboard for periods between 24 hours and 12 months. The support desk can also help arrange a permanent block.

Service availability

The platform is provided on an "as is, as available" basis. Scheduled maintenance windows are announced in advance inside the dashboard. We are not liable for losses arising from emergency maintenance, outages caused by third-party providers or events outside our reasonable control.

Termination

You may close your account at any time from the dashboard or by emailing info@ridgefundastead-au.com. We may also terminate access in the case of a regulatory order, suspected fraud or breach of these conditions, in line with applicable Australian law.

Contact

Questions about these Conditions of Use can be sent to info@ridgefundastead-au.com. The team responds within five business days for general queries and one business day for compliance requests.
